Your own numbers. An empty field means that rule is off: the relay presets none.

3. Signal sources

Who may send signals. Each source has its own secret, which lives in .env, never here.

4. Notifications

Where the relay tells you what happened: Telegram, Discord or a webhook.

Stop trading

Close all closes every position of this deployment at market and leaves the relay running.

Emergency stop closes every position at market, refuses new entries and stops the engine. It stays in force after a restart.

Resume lifts an emergency stop: new entries are taken again.
